# Client Recommendations

## Client A: Startup Company

### Recommended Platform: **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**

### Recommendation:
GCP is the best choice for this startup because of its competitive pricing, especially with sustained use discounts and preemptible instances. GCP's extensive free tier allows the startup to launch with minimal upfront costs. The startup can use GCP's managed services to scale quickly as they grow. Additionally, GCP's strong focus on modern application development and Kubernetes support aligns with agile startup methodologies.

### Recommended Services:
- **Compute Engine** - For scalable virtual machines
- **Cloud Firestore** - NoSQL database for mobile app backend
- **Cloud Run** - Serverless container platform for microservices

---

## Client B: University

### Recommended Platform: **Microsoft Azure**

### Recommendation:
Azure is the clear choice for this university because of its seamless integration with their existing Microsoft infrastructure, including Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory. The university can easily extend their on-premises Active Directory to Azure AD for hybrid identity management. Azure's education pricing and commitment to compliance and security standards make it suitable for educational institutions handling student data.

### Recommended Services:
- **Azure Virtual Machines** - For migrating Windows Server workloads
- **Azure Active Directory** - Extending identity management to the cloud
- **Azure Files** - File storage compatible with existing network drives

---

## Client C: AI Research Company

### Recommended Platform: **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**

### Recommendation:
GCP is the strongest choice for this AI research company due to its leadership in machine learning and AI technologies. GCP offers specialized hardware like TPUs (Tensor Processing Units) for high-performance computing. The company can leverage Vertex AI for the entire ML workflow, from data preparation to model deployment. GCP's BigQuery also provides powerful data analytics capabilities for research data.

### Recommended Services:
- **Vertex AI** - End-to-end machine learning platform
- **Compute Engine with TPUs** - High-performance computing for AI training
- **BigQuery** - Data warehousing for research data analysis

---

## Client D: Global E-Commerce Company

### Recommended Platform: **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**

### Recommendation:
AWS is the ideal choice for this multinational e-commerce company due to its unmatched global infrastructure and mature services for high-availability applications. AWS offers the most extensive global presence with 32 regions, ensuring low latency for customers worldwide. Auto-scaling capabilities with Elastic Load Balancing ensure the application can handle traffic spikes during peak shopping seasons. Additionally, AWS's mature ecosystem offers comprehensive security and compliance services.

### Recommended Services:
- **Amazon EC2 with Auto Scaling** - For elastic computing capacity
- **Amazon RDS Multi-AZ** - For highly available databases
- **Amazon CloudFront** - Global content delivery network
- **Elastic Load Balancer** - For distributing traffic across instances

## Multi-Cloud Decision Matrix

| Business Requirement | Recommended Platform | Justification |
|----------------------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Startup Company** | **Google Cloud Platform** | Cost-effective with competitive pricing, strong free tier, and modern development tools |
